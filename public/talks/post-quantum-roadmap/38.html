The focus areas · Focus 9

Message Level Encryption (MLE)

  • You may be protecting messages using a public/private key pair.
  • The code that processes decryption will need to be modularized to support current and Post Quantum algorithms.
  • You may need a configuration switch per entity to control which encryption is used.
  • You will need to plan to get new keys, and possibly support dual keys per entity.
  • Lastly, plan for when a Post Quantum algorithm also needs to be replaced due to a weakness.
  • Planning for when you are compromised, not if, can save a significant amount of time and money down the road.
