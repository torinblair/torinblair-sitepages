What is post quantum cryptography

  • A set of encryption and signature algorithms that cannot be broken by quantum computers, even those running Shor’s or Grover’s algorithm.
  • Uses lattice problems rather than factorization of primes.
  • Runs on classical computers, but is resistant to quantum computers.
  • Algorithms
    • ML-KEM – Key encapsulation
    • ML-DSA – Digital signature creation and validation
    • SLH-DSA – Stateless hash based signatures
    • FN-DSA – Small signatures with fast verification (draft / expected)
