The focus areas · Focus 3

Certificates

  • Know when hybrid Post Quantum certificates will be available from your vendor – the realistic timeline is 2027 to 2029.
  • There are two methods of certificate issuance:
    • Dual certificates for the same identity – one classical certificate, one PQC certificate
      • Supports maximum compatibility
    • Composite certificate – signatures from classical and PQC algorithms are merged into one composite signature
      • Tougher to forge
  • Plan on testing once those certificates are available.
  • If you self-issue certificates from internal systems such as Active Directory Certificate Services, HashiCorp Vault or Keyfactor, the support timeline may be different.
