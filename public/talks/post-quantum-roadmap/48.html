NIST standards

  • NIST FIPS 204 – Module-Lattice-Based Digital Signature Algorithm (ML-DSA), August 2024
  • NIST FIPS 205 – Stateless Hash-Based Digital Signature Algorithm (SLH-DSA), August 2024
  • NIST IR 8413 – Status Report on the Third Round of the Post-Quantum Cryptography Standardization Process

Foundational research

Software and libraries

  • Open Quantum Safe (liboqs) – C library for quantum-safe cryptographic algorithms (openquantumsafe.org)
  • PQCrypto – Post-quantum cryptography library for Rust (crates.io/crates/pqcrypto)
