The longer term

What about already harvested and stored information

  • We know we already have a problem.
  • Telecommunications networks have been lurked on, and messages have been intercepted and stored by various governments.
  • This information can be decrypted once the private key has been derived by a quantum machine.
  • For most of us, this will be our financial information, which is already half available on the dark web thanks to other system compromises.
  • For some businesses, this will be other keys or secrets used to protect your systems.
  • For some entities, this will be important strategic information.
